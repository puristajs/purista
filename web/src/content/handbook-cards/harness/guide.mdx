title: AI Harness Guide
description: A progressive implementation path from a first typed agent to production operations.
---

The AI Harness is a standalone, self-hosted TypeScript runtime. It gives your
application one typed boundary for model providers, tools, skills, agents,
workflows, state, safety controls, and OpenTelemetry. It does not own your
HTTP API, queue, authorization, or business data.

## Follow this path

1. Start with [Overview](/handbook/harness/guide/overview/) to choose the
   correct runtime boundary.
2. Complete [Quickstart](/handbook/harness/guide/quickstart/) with one model,
   one agent, and one session.
3. Add [Models & Configuration](/handbook/harness/guide/models-and-configuration/)
   and [Tools & Skills](/handbook/harness/guide/tools-and-skills/) only as the
   use case requires them.
4. Use [Agents, Workflows & State](/handbook/harness/guide/agents-workflows-state/)
   when work needs orchestration or durability.
5. Add [Guardrails & Governance](/handbook/harness/guide/guardrails-governance/),
   then prove it with [Testing & Evaluations](/handbook/harness/guide/testing-and-evaluations/).
6. Finish with [Observability & Operations](/handbook/harness/guide/observability-operations/)
   and [Adapters, Durability & Reference](/handbook/harness/adapters-durability-reference/).

If you are attaching the runtime to a PURISTA service, command, queue, or
stream, continue with [Harness Integration](/handbook/blocks/agent-pattern/harness-integration/)
after the standalone quickstart. That section owns the framework boundary; this
chapter owns Harness behavior.
