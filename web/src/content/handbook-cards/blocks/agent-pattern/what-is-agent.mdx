---
title: What is a mounted Harness target?
description: Understand native Harness agents and workflows published at PURISTA service addresses.
---

An agent or workflow is defined with `@purista/harness`. A PURISTA service may
publish selected targets at versioned EventBridge addresses. The definition
stays provider-neutral; the service supplies trusted identity, business guards,
resources, and runtime adapters.

Continue with [Architecture and lifecycle](/handbook/framework/build-ai-powered-services/architecture-and-lifecycle/).
