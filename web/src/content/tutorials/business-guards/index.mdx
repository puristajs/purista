A valid session tells us who called. It does not tell us which account that
person may use. This chapter adds business authorization to the `Transaction`
service with PURISTA command guards.

The example stays small. Alex may read and record transactions for the
operating account. Sam may read that account but may not record transactions
for it. Sam has full access to the review account. Both people are valid users.
The command guard makes the business decision.
