---
title: Find stale guidance
description: Treat a newer source revision as a signal to propose review, never as an automatic replacement.
order: 183
status: published
---

The changed source makes the old wiki revision stale. This small deterministic
checkpoint does not use a model to rewrite the canonical page. It records the
new source revision, then creates a reviewable proposal with that exact number.
In a later AI extension, retrieval and analysis must remain scoped before a
proposal is created.
