This chapter builds a small classifier with content controls. An input rail
blocks one instruction-override pattern before the provider runs. An output
rail removes long card-like digit sequences from the final structured reason
before it reaches the caller.

Guardrails answer a content question: may this exact value enter or leave the
agent? They do not authenticate the caller and do not decide whether a
principal may perform a business action. PURISTA business guards keep that
separate responsibility.

```text title="Guardrail placement"
validated agent input
  -> input content rail
  -> model provider
  -> schema-valid final candidate
  -> output content rail
  -> PURISTA caller
```

You will generate the complete PURISTA and Harness structure, install the
optional package, define typed actions and ordered flows, attach them to the
native agent, protect its published address, and prove that blocked content or
denied business access never reaches the model.

`examples/banking/chapters/ai-guardrails`.
