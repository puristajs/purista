title: Understand the current limits
description: Know what the local in-memory metrics proof does not demonstrate before using it as an operations design.

---

The test uses `createMemoryMetricsRecorder()` and passes it to the local
application through `createBankingApplication({ metricsRecorder })`. This
proves that the service emits the declared records in a real HTTP, subscription,
and queue-worker flow.

It does not prove any of the following:

- export to OpenTelemetry, Prometheus, or a cloud product;
- trace correlation across an event or a queue;
- redaction of logs, automatic trace fields, or a remote exporter payload;
- behavior when an exporter is slow, unavailable, or flushing during shutdown;
- durability, retry, or recovery after the in-memory bridges restart.

The counters are useful local evidence for the chapter's exact behavior. They
are not a compliance audit trail or a production observability configuration.
