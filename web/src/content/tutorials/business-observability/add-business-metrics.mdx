---
title: Add bounded business metrics
description: Use the two counters for operational outcomes without turning banking identifiers into metric labels.
order: 94
---

The current metrics answer two narrow questions:

| Counter | Allowed attributes | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `example.bank.review.signals` | `rule_version`, `outcome` | A training threshold was recorded or was below the threshold. |
| `example.bank.background.jobs` | `job`, `outcome` | A statement or reconciliation request entered its local queue. |

This is intentionally less detail than a business audit record. Account IDs,
principal IDs, transaction IDs, tenant IDs, request IDs, and customer text are
not permitted labels. Such values can create thousands of distinct series and
may reveal data that a metric system should not carry.

Metrics do not make a repeated event idempotent. The monitoring code and its
store still own the business effect. Metrics only observe the result of that
work.

Next, review the limits that prevent this local proof from being mistaken for a
full observability system.
