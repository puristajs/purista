---
title: Make the plan and evidence visible
description: Store a short work plan and approved evidence instead of hidden reasoning.
order: 172
status: published
---

The memo has three small plan steps: collect approved sources, draft a
proposal, and validate its claims. It then records only source IDs and short
summaries needed to review the proposal. This is a work artifact, not a record
of a model’s private reasoning.

```ts title="examples/banking/src/memos/service.ts"
plan: ['Collect approved sources', 'Draft a short proposal', 'Validate claims against citations'],
citations: [{ sourceId: 'fee-policy-v1', summary: '...' }],
```

The source set is fixed in this first example. A real retrieval step must apply
its own collection and tenant authorization before it produces any excerpt.
