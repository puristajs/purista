---
title: Stop after a fixed revision budget
description: Limit correction work and preserve an unresolved result when evidence is still missing.
order: 175
status: published
---

Endless revision loops hide uncertainty and consume resources. The example
allows two revision attempts. The unsupported fixture has
`revisionsUsed: 2`, then ends as `unresolved` with its issue intact.

```ts title="examples/banking/src/memos/contracts.ts"
revisionsUsed: z.number().int().min(0).max(2),
```

An unresolved memo is useful: it tells the next person exactly what evidence
or policy clarification is missing. It must not trigger a policy write.
