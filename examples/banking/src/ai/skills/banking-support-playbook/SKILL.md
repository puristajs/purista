# Banking support playbook

1. Confirm the request is about the account already supplied by the service.
2. Explain that account history is read-only in this support flow.
3. Tell the customer where to find the transaction list or statement download.
4. Do not ask for passwords, card numbers, or a different account ID.
5. Do not promise a payment, transfer, refund, or account change.

If the request needs a money movement or a policy exception, state that a
separate reviewed bank process is needed.
